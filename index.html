GOOD TO KNOW

A few
answers.

This is an early storefront preview. Here is what is and is not ready yet.

Are these real products and final prices?+

No. The current listings and prices are examples used to preview the design. They must be replaced with verified product, supplier and pricing information before accepting orders.

Can customers pay on this website?+

Not yet. This version does not collect payments. The bag can prepare a product enquiry for WhatsApp after a valid business number is configured.

Can I order or track a shipment now?+

No. Supplier connection, stock checks, shipping, order confirmation and tracking have not been configured. Availability and delivery details must be confirmed before any sale.

How do I contact ANAX?+

Contact details need to be added by the store owner. The WhatsApp enquiry button will stay disabled until a valid number is entered in the setup section of the site code.
